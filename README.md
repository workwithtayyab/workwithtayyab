A responsive business website for RR AquaTech Solutions that showcases its water treatment and industrial services, strengthens its online presence, and generates customer inquiries. It has dedicated sections for company profile, services, projects and contact details, plus interactive navigation, smooth animations, and a contact inquiry form with backend email integration.

- 📱 Responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile
- 🏢 Company profile, services and projects sections
- ✉️ Inquiry form with email integration for lead generation
- ✨ Interactive navigation and smooth animations
